# Supermarket Sales Data Analysis Project

## 1. Project Overview
This project analyses supermarket sales data to understand business performance, customer behaviour, product performance, and profitability.

The goal is to transform raw data into meaningful insights that can help management make informed business decisions.

## 2. Dataset
- **Dataset:** Supermarket Sales
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/datascikhan/supermarket/data

## 3. Project Objectives
- Analyse supermarket sales performance.
- Identify top-selling products.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Evaluate product profitability.
- Identify sales trends.
- Provide recommendations to improve business performance.

## 4. Business Questions

### Sales Analysis
1. What is the total revenue generated?
2. What is the total number of transactions?
3. What is the average sales value?
4. Which month recorded the highest sales?
5. Which month recorded the lowest sales?
6. What are the sales trends over time?

### Product Analysis
7. Which products generate the highest revenue?
8. Which products generate the lowest revenue?
9. Which product categories have the highest sales?
10. Which categories contribute the most to profit?
11. Which products perform poorly?

### Customer Analysis
12. How many unique customers are in the dataset?
13. Who are the top customers based on purchases?
14. Which customer groups generate the highest revenue?
15. What are the customer purchasing patterns?

### Profitability Analysis
16. What is the total profit generated?
17. Which products generate the highest profit?
18. Which products generate the lowest profit?
19. Which categories have negative profitability?
20. What factors may be responsible for low profitability?

### Business Recommendations
21. How can the supermarket increase sales?
22. How can the supermarket improve profitability?
23. Which products or categories need more attention?
24. What strategies can improve customer retention?
